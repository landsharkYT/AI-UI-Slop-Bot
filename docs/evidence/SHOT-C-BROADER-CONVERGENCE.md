# Shot C: Broader Convergence Calibration

Status: local implementation and read-only dogfood complete; customer calibration pending.

Date: 2026-07-30

Rule pack `1.0.0-beta.5` adds two source-evidenced rules:

- Framework Default Convergence identifies a neutral stock framework recipe recurring across at least three component owners.
- Control Surface Homogenization identifies compact outlined surface traits recurring across at least eight elements and at least three structural roles.

Public seam tests establish positive and acceptable-negative boundaries, House Style neutralization, and class-renaming invariance. The implementation resolves control traits from both Tailwind utilities and statically referenced simple plain-CSS classes.
