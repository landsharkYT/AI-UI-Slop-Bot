# Frozen coding-agent cleanup protocol

Predeclare at least ten fresh Full V1 trial IDs, repository revisions, Refactoring Briefs, intended Slop Patterns, Preservation Obligations, House Style constraints, agent/model assignments, capability mappings, verification commands, and blind-review assignments. Cover at least two independent model families and include a small comparable overlap set. Run work only on disposable branches or copies.

The agent receives the proposed Refactoring Brief and ordinary repository access. For paired capability trials, one run receives the brief alone and the other receives the same brief plus the predeclared capability mapping. Do not reveal scanner scores as an optimization target.

Record exact agent/model version, available tools, starting revision, brief version, prompts, elapsed effort, changed files, verification results, preservation results, corrective prompts, and rendered before/after references. A factual answer about an unknown Preservation Obligation or supplied House Style constraint is non-corrective. Restating/changing the design concern, pointing to a missed target, requesting repair of broken behavior, or supplying an omitted remediation decision is material corrective prompting and fails that condition.

A trial passes only when every Preservation Obligation is independently verified, the cleanup is preferred as less convergent, and no material corrective prompt was required. At least eight of ten must pass.

Freeze each before/after pair before outcomes are reviewed. Give each pair to at least three external React maintainers in randomized order without revealing which is the cleanup. Reviewers judge which version is more deliberate, distinctive, and appropriate to the stated purpose and House Style. At least two-thirds must prefer the cleanup, alongside Design Authority approval and independent behavior/accessibility checks. Preserve ties, abstentions, ambiguous obligations, and failures.
